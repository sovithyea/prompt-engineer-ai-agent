How your data is used

When you click Enhance Prompt, the text currently in the box is sent to Prompt Polish's own server, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to generate clarifying questions or a rewritten prompt. Nothing is logged or stored — not by Prompt Polish, not on our server.

Nothing is read or sent at any other time. Full details: privacy policy.

If Prompt Polish is too vague to rewrite confidently, it'll ask up to two quick clarifying questions first.
